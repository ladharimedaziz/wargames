Coats of arms, named by ISO numeric country id (Kosovo: XKX).
Rendered to WebP from the SVGs in the npm package "coat-of-arms" 6.4.0 (MIT licence).
Countries that package leaves out (Iran, Turkey, Syria, Cuba, Taiwan, Singapore, Laos, Moldova, North Macedonia,
Tanzania, Palestine, Vatican, Gambia, Micronesia, Central African Republic, Congo, DR Congo) show their flag on a
shield, drawn from the npm package "country-flag-icons" (MIT licence).
